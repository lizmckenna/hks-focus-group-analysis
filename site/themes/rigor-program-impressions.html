Academic Rigor: Expectations Met and Unmet

20 utterances across 4 programs

"I think it oftentimes exceeded my expectations in terms of rigor and also coherence between the different classes. I've been really impressed by the rigor, maybe too much at times where the pace has been difficult to manage." MPAID participant · MPAID positive experience

Analysis

Rigor is the theme that most sharply divides along program lines. MPAID students find it appropriately challenging and well-designed. MPP1s describe it as 'extremely underwhelming' with inconsistent standards. MCMPAs appreciate the democratic classroom culture but see discussions as sometimes performative rather than substantive. The common thread: rigor is not a simple dial. Students want the right level of challenge, clearly signaled expectations, and accountability for both preparation and engagement.

What's Working

MPAID students are the most satisfied with rigor, praising both the challenge level and the coherence across courses. MCMPAs value the openness and democratic nature of HKS classroom culture compared to prior educational experiences. Several students across programs noted strong individual courses and professors.

Areas for Improvement

The heterogeneity of student preparation creates a leveling problem: some students find courses too easy, others too hard, and the middle is unsatisfying for both. Large participation grades in large classes produce quantity over quality in discussions. Some students describe being 'spoon-fed' administratively in ways that undermine professional development.
