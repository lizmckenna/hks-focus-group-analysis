Teaching Quality & Faculty Investment

22 utterances across 5 programs

"My experience with my professors here has been deeply inspiring. No matter whatever priorities they had, they treated me, their students, as their top priority." MCMPA participant · Faculty investment at its best

Analysis

Students distinguish clearly between the best and worst teaching experiences at HKS, and the gap is wide. The best professors are described as deeply prepared, pedagogically intentional, and invested in student learning. But quality is inconsistent, and students want to understand what incentives and structures exist to hold teaching quality accountable. The question of how HKS evaluates and develops its professors came up explicitly.

What's Working

Faculty are broadly seen as professional and knowledgeable. Multiple students described individual professors as 'deeply inspiring' in their preparation and pedagogical design. The MPAID program received especially strong praise for interconnecting courses and managing discussions effectively.

Areas for Improvement

There is wide variability across professors. Some appear to have designed courses around their research interests rather than student learning outcomes. Students want more pedagogical investment and wonder whether HKS has mechanisms to address teaching quality, particularly for discussion facilitation, which many professors handle poorly.
