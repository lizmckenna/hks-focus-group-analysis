Emotional Landscape

Sentiment analysis across all focus groups

Sentiment scores are tools for finding interesting quotes and mapping emotional terrain, not for reducing experience to numbers. Every chart below shows actual quotes on hover. Scores range from -1 (most negative) to +1 (most positive). A score around 0.5 typically indicates warm, appreciative language; a score near -0.5 indicates frustration or criticism. See Methodology for calibration notes on VADER.

Sentiment Arcs

Each chart tracks the emotional trajectory through a focus group session. The line shows a rolling average; individual dots are single utterances. Hover over any dot to see the actual quote and its sentiment score. Look for sharp dips (moments of frustration or critique) and peaks (moments of enthusiasm or agreement). For example, most sessions start neutral-to-positive during introductions, then shift as substantive topics emerge.

Sentiment by Theme

These violin plots show the full emotional range within each theme. The width shows how many utterances have that sentiment level; the box shows the middle 50%; the line shows the mean. Wider violins mean more diversity of feeling about that topic.
